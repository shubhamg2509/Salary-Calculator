What this tool assumes — read before trusting the number

  • Pluxee/Other Benefits are always funded in full first (up to what you opt in); LTA takes its 10%-of-Gross-Salary target only if there's room left, otherwise it shrinks to cover the shortfall. Special Allowance only shows a number when there's genuine surplus beyond that.
  • Employer NPS % is a manual input, not a fixed company policy figure — confirm your actual contribution rate with your employer's payroll/HR team.
  • LTA exemption (Old Regime) assumes you hold valid travel bills for the claim; it is not automatically verified.
  • 80D is shown as a single simplified field rather than the separate self/parent/senior-citizen sub-limits.
  • Surcharge marginal relief is now modelled at each threshold (₹50L, ₹1Cr, ₹2Cr, and ₹5Cr for Old Regime) — crossing a threshold by ₹1 won't spike your tax by lakhs.
  • The ₹7,50,000 ceiling on combined Employer PF + Employer NPS (+ Superannuation, if any) is a combined cap under Sec 17(2)(vii) — not a per-scheme limit. Verified against multiple current sources including post-Budget-2026 guidance; this hasn't changed.
  • Home loan interest: the ₹2,00,000 cap on offsetting salary income applies whether the property is self-occupied or let-out (Sec 71(3A)) — this tool doesn't collect rental income, so it can't model the uncapped let-out interest deduction against rental income itself, only the capped set-off against salary.
  • This tool projects a full FY as if your current Gross Salary applied for the whole period you're prorated for. If your Gross Salary changed mid-year, your actual TDS won't match this exactly — real payroll blends tax already deducted at the old rate with projected tax at the new rate, which this tool can't replicate without your revision history.
  • This is a planning estimate, not a substitute for your Form 16 / a tax advisor.
