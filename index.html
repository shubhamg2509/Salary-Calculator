What this tool assumes

  1. 01Pluxee and Other Benefits are always funded in full first, up to what you opted in for. LTA takes its 10%‑of‑gross target only if there's room left, otherwise it shrinks to cover the shortfall. Special Allowance shows a number only when there's genuine surplus beyond that.
  2. 02Employer NPS % is a manual input, not a fixed company policy figure — confirm your actual contribution rate with payroll.
  3. 03The LTA exemption under the Old Regime assumes you hold valid travel bills for the claim. It is not automatically verified.
  4. 0480D is a single simplified field rather than the separate self, parent and senior‑citizen sub‑limits.
  5. 05Surcharge marginal relief is modelled at every threshold — ₹50L, ₹1Cr, ₹2Cr, and ₹5Cr for the Old Regime — so crossing one by ₹1 won't spike your tax by lakhs.
  6. 06The ₹7,50,000 ceiling on employer PF + employer NPS (+ superannuation, if any) is a combined cap under Sec 17(2)(vii), not a per‑scheme limit.
  7. 07Home‑loan interest: the ₹2,00,000 cap on offsetting salary income applies whether the property is self‑occupied or let‑out (Sec 71(3A)). This tool doesn't collect rental income, so it models only the capped set‑off against salary.
  8. 08It projects a full financial year as if your current gross salary applied throughout. If your salary changed mid‑year, real payroll blends tax already deducted at the old rate with projected tax at the new one, which this can't replicate without your revision history.
  9. 09This is a planning estimate, not a substitute for your Form 16 or a tax adviser.
